HEADACHE

A headache is pain or discomfort in the head, scalp, or neck.
Common types include tension headaches and migraine.
Headaches may occur with factors such as stress, lack of sleep,
dehydration, or illness.

A severe or unusual headache, especially when associated with
confusion, fainting, weakness, difficulty speaking, vision problems,
or other serious symptoms may require urgent medical attention.

FEVER

Fever is a temporary increase in body temperature that can occur
when the body responds to infection or other causes.

Common symptoms associated with fever may include feeling hot,
chills, sweating, weakness, headache, and body aches.

Persistent, very high, or concerning fever should be evaluated
by a healthcare professional, particularly when accompanied by
serious symptoms.

PARACETAMOL

Paracetamol is a medicine commonly used to relieve pain and reduce
fever.

Users should follow the instructions on the medicine label or the
advice of a qualified healthcare professional. The appropriate dose
can depend on factors such as age, formulation, and individual
circumstances.

A healthcare professional or pharmacist should be consulted when
there is uncertainty about dosage, interactions, contraindications,
or safe use.

HYPERTENSION

Hypertension means persistently elevated blood pressure.

High blood pressure may not always cause noticeable symptoms.
Long-term uncontrolled hypertension can increase the risk of
cardiovascular and other health problems.

Blood pressure concerns should be discussed with a qualified
healthcare professional.
